About关于我

I am a senior at Wellesley College, studying Mathematics and Women's and Gender Studies. My honors thesis asks what happens to Chinese-language queer women's content on short-video platforms — how long posts stay up, which ones stop being visible, and what that is like for the people who made them.

I work on it with Prof. Elena Creef (Women's and Gender Studies) and Prof. Eni Mustafaraj (Computer Science). Before this I spent a year on a related project about T/P/H labelling culture on Douyin, building and hand-coding a dataset of 600+ videos.

I also work as a research assistant at Yale with Prof. Emma Zang, where I run semi-structured interviews with Chinese respondents about family and fertility, and at the MIT Sloan Superminds Lab with Prof. Thomas Malone on how people and AI systems review each other's work (poster at CI/HCOMP 2026).

Current study — looking for interviewees当前研究 · 访谈招募

Creators' own accounts of posting this content

I am interviewing people who post Chinese-language queer women's content on short-video platforms, about what posting is like: whether you think about what might not be allowed, what has happened to things you posted, and whether any of it changed how you post.
